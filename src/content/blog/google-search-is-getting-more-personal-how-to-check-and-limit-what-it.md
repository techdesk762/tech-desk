Google Search is about to know more about you, but only if you let it. A new batch of connections lets Google's AI Mode pull from your payment cards, files and chats to give answers that fit your life. Each one is optional, and you can switch each off.

This is a good moment to check what is connected to your Google account. It takes a few minutes.

## What this means for you

AI Mode is Google's chat-style search, where you ask a question and get a written answer. With "Personal Intelligence" switched on, it can use information from your other apps to make those answers more specific.

Gmail, Calendar and Photos were already on the list. Google Wallet is now being added, with Google Drive and the Gemini app due within a few weeks.

Here is what that could look like, based on Google's own examples:

- **Wallet:** With your cards linked, AI Mode can suggest how to get more from cash-back categories, travel perks and reward points. Google also says it can help you plan financial milestones and find ways to trim routine spending.
- **Drive:** Answers can take into account information spread across documents, spreadsheets and PDFs. Google's example is building a tax-season preparation plan.
- **Gemini app:** Context can carry between the Gemini app and AI Mode. An idea you explore in one can continue in the other.

Google says Wallet access is read-only. AI Mode can't move, manage or invest your money.

Separately, AI Overviews (the short summaries at the top of results) and AI Mode will use your recent search context. If you've been researching a trip, you can start a new thread later without explaining it all again.

The trade-off is simple. More connections can mean more useful answers. They also mean more of your personal information is in play when you search.

## What changed

Google is widening the set of apps that Personal Intelligence in Search can use.

- Wallet connection begins rolling out on Nov 23, 2026, for AI Mode. It also works in the Gemini app.
- Drive and Gemini app connections follow within a few weeks.
- Search history could already be linked to Personal Intelligence in Gemini. Soon the Gemini app can also be linked to AI Mode.
- A "Personalization" icon is coming under tailored responses. It will show what context AI Mode used.
- You will be able to correct that context by telling AI Mode in the conversation.

## What to do today

1. **Find the controls.** Google says to open Search Personalization, choose Connected Apps, then pick the apps you want. The notes don't say where Search Personalization sits in your account, so check Google's [announcement post](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/google-search-personal-intelligence-apps/) for the latest guidance.
2. **Review what's connected.** Look at which apps are already linked. Gmail, Calendar and Photos are the existing options.
3. **Decide app by app.** Ask yourself whether each connection would actually help. Wallet might suit someone who wants to get more from reward points. It might not suit someone who prefers to keep payment details out of search.
4. **Switch off what you don't want.** Google says you can turn any connection on or off whenever you like.
5. **Check Search Settings.** Google says personalization can be adjusted or disabled there at any time. If you want less tailoring overall, this is the place to look.
6. **Watch for the new icon.** When the "Personalization" icon appears under an answer, tap it to see what context AI Mode used.
7. **Correct mistakes in the chat.** If AI Mode has something out of date, tell it. Google's example is saying you are no longer vegetarian.

## Our take

Our take: the most useful part of this announcement is not the new apps. It is the visibility. A "Personalization" icon that shows what context shaped an answer is the kind of feature that makes personal AI easier to trust. If an answer seems oddly specific, you can see why. If it's wrong, you can fix it.

The Wallet link is the one to think hardest about. The read-only promise is reassuring, since the AI can't move or invest money. But reading is still access. Whether it's worth it depends on how much you value tips on rewards and spending against how comfortable you are with that data being used in search. Those tips are only as good as the data behind them, and we haven't seen how well they work in practice.

Our recommendation: don't switch everything on at once. Connect one app, use it for a week or two, and see whether the answers get better. Keep the rest off until you have a clear reason to add them. Because each connection can be switched off, trying one costs you little.

## What we don't know yet

- **Where exactly the settings live.** Google names "Search Personalization" and "Connected Apps," but the notes don't give the full path through your account.
- **Who gets access, and when.** Wallet starts rolling out on Nov 23, 2026. We don't know how quickly it reaches everyone, or whether availability varies by country or account type.
- **Exact timing for Drive and the Gemini app.** Google says "within a few weeks," with no firm date.
- **How the Personalization icon will look and how detailed it will be.** It is described as coming, not live.
- **How accurate the tailored answers will be.** Google gives examples, such as tax planning and reward tips. We haven't tested them and can't say how reliable they are.
- **What happens to your data over time.** The notes cover read-only access and on/off controls. They don't say how long context is kept or how it is stored. For those details, check Google's own documentation.
